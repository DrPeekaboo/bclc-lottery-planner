    History analysis

    Counts how often each number was drawn and tests whether the draws look like fair random draws. Past frequencies never make a number more or less likely next time.

    Number generator

    Builds lines within your budget. Every line must have at least three numbers above 31, no three-number runs or evenly spaced triples, more than one decade and slip column, at most one multiple of 7, at most one number containing a 7, and any two lines share at most two numbers.

    Favourite-number wheel

    Pick 10–15 favourites. A wheel aims to guarantee at least a 3-number match whenever 3 of your favourites are drawn. The guarantee is shown only after every triple is checked against the final lines and the lines fit your budget.
